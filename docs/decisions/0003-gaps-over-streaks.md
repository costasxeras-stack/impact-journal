# 0003: Show skill gaps first, keep streaks small

**Status:** accepted, 0.1.0

## Context

Habit apps lean on streaks. For a review, the more useful signal is what the record is missing.

## Decision

The dashboard leads with skills and reach, marks skills with no entry in 6 months in red, and shows weeks in a row as one small number.

## Tradeoff

- Pushes the user to act on gaps before the review, not after.
- Less of a daily pull to open the app. A 14 day nudge covers that.
- Skills are the user's own list, so gaps depend on choosing good skills.
